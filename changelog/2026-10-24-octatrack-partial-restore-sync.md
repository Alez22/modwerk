Octatrack base (USB, beta): when the Octatrack started and couldn't put back one of your kept modules, every sync after that was refused with "The Octatrack can't run this module file" until a restart brought all of them back. The base now counts only the modules that came back as your installed set, so you can sync again straight away, including the missing module. Your memory card keeps the full set until your next sync, so the next start still tries the missing module. Why the module was refused at the start (a tester's Ringmod SC) is still unknown.
