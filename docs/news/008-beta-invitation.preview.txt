You’re invited to the Modwerk beta

Hey guys,

over the past weeks I’ve been working on a big Octatrack update – and it’s finally at a point where I’d love for some of you to try it before it’s out for everyone.

It brings a new way to load modules over USB: you install the Modwerk base once from the card, and after that modules go straight from the site onto your Octatrack – no card, no reboot. 19 modules already work this way, including POLY8 and Air Chorus, which are only in the beta for now.

It also changes how FX get loaded onto the DSP chip. They’re loaded on demand now, and tracks on the same DSP core share one copy of the code, so the DSP memory is used a lot better. On top of that the Octatrack runs a self-test after every sync and you decide if you keep it or undo it, your modules come back by themselves after a restart, it tells you when a project uses a module that’s missing, and all the stock FX stay where they are.

You’ll need an Octatrack MKII, your OS 1.40C file, a USB cable and Chrome or Edge on a computer. I’m keeping the beta to 50 people for now.

Join the beta (https://modwerk.app/#beta/join/00000000000000000000000000000000)

One important thing: it’s still a beta, and the modules are not safe to use in a live setting (yet). You’ll find the details and risks on the site.

So far I’ve only tested it on my own MKII, so I really need your feedback – come say hi in the development Discord and let me know what you think.

Thank you,
Jannik
