You’re invited to the Modwerk beta

Hey guys,

the past weeks I’ve been working on a big Octatrack update, and I’d love for you to try it before it’s out for everyone.

The main thing: you can now load modules over USB. You install the Modwerk base once from the card – after that, modules go straight from the site onto your Octatrack, without the card and without a reboot. 19 modules already work this way, including POLY8 and Air Chorus, which are only in the beta for now.

What else is new:

- DSP modules get loaded onto the DSP chip on demand, and tracks on the same DSP core share one copy of the code – so the DSP memory is used a lot better
- your modules come back by themselves after a restart
- after every sync the Octatrack runs a self-test, then you keep it or undo it
- if a project uses a module that isn’t on the Octatrack, it tells you which one
- the stock effects stay – none of them get replaced

You’ll need an Octatrack MKII, your OS 1.40C file, a USB cable and Chrome or Edge on a computer. The beta is limited to 50 people.

Join the beta (https://modwerk.app/#beta/join/00000000000000000000000000000000)

It’s a beta, so stuff can still break – you’ll find the details and risks on the site. Come say hi in the development Discord and let me know what you think!

Thank you,
